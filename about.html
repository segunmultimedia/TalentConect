The Genesis

How It All Began

J Mime Ministry began in 2015 in Sunyani, Ghana, through a dance ministry known as F6. What started as dance soon grew into a deeper calling—mime.

We began using mime as a powerful tool for evangelism, reaching out to communities and villages, sharing the Gospel beyond words. Along the way, we also embraced preaching to further impact lives and spread God's message.

By God's grace, we have ministered on various platforms across Ghana, including major programs attended by national leaders such as the Vice President. We were also privileged to be the first African recipients of a Gospel Mime International Award at the 2021 Gospel Choice Awards in Atlanta, Georgia, USA.

Today, we remain committed to using mime to inspire, empower, and draw people closer to God.
